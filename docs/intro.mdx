# Mathematics of Chia

This textbook explains the mathematics needed to understand the Chia
blockchain. It starts from first principles and builds toward the structures
used in proof of space, proof of time, finite fields, elliptic curves, BLS
signatures, and verifiable delay functions.

The intended reader is a motivated programmer who wants clear definitions,
worked examples, and a careful bridge from mathematical ideas to Chia protocol
concepts.

The chapter files are intentionally small at this stage. Content will be added
gradually so the structure stays easy to review and build.
