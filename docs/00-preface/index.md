# Welcome to *Mathematics of Chia*

*Mathematics of Chia* is a web textbook about the mathematical ideas behind the
Chia blockchain. The book begins with foundations and builds toward the
cryptography and protocol mechanisms used in Chia.

## Roadmap

The textbook is organized into chapters on foundations, number theory, algebra,
elliptic curves, pairings, BLS signatures, class groups, VDFs, proof of space,
Chia consensus, and CLVM transactions.

## Intended audience

The intended reader is comfortable with proof-based mathematics or is willing to
learn it while studying applied cryptography. Each chapter will eventually include
motivation, definitions, examples, exercises, and references for further study.
